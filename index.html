About Me

Hello! Once again, I am Ka Wong! I am from Chicago, Illinois and currently a undergrad student at Northwestern university studying Computer Science. I am in my second year of study and expect to graduate in June 2020. I was born on September 20, 1997. I learn Web Development during my free time from The Odin Project and Free Code Camp. I am a fan of pan crust pizza, ping pong, and the oxford comma.

After changing majors a few times, I found CS coursework to be most rewarding and intriguing. I cannot stress enough that I wished I have been exposed to programming before starting college.

It's fun I can type something out, in a bizarre syntactical language a computer can interpret and translate into actions. It's creative where small pieces of code can add up to a functional any-purpose application. And it's satisfying knowing the feeling of debugging for hours / days / weeks to successfully fix a bug or optimize a program.

I program as a hobby, and look forward to working professionally in the field.
